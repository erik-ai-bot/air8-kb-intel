Appendix — Air8 Financing Products Reference (shown once, not per-card)

ARP — Accounts Receivable Purchase

Air8 purchases approved receivables. Eligibility: existing ARP track record + confirmed buyer + clean documentation. Decision gate: buyer quality + receivable non-dispute history.

OBF — Order-Based Financing

Financing against confirmed PO. Eligibility: confirmed PO + existing buyer relationship. Decision gate: PO confirmation + buyer credit quality + COO documentation.

PreShip — Pre-Shipment Financing

Funds production ahead of shipment. Blocked: CMT with buyer-nominated fabric + no confirmed PO + UFLPA unresolved.

RT2C — Request to Charge (Supplier)

Air8 validates receivable package for Buyside Capital financing. Blocked: CMT suppliers where buyer controls fabric and pricing.

ESG Finance

Linked to ESG/certification triggers. Blocked: ESG finance without separate ESG trigger and IFC criteria proof. Not triggered by Section 301 alone.

Key Decision Gates — Independence Rule

Every financing decision requires: (1) confirmed buyer and order, (2) UFLPA readiness + traceability + supply chain transparency, (3) pricing terms (FOB vs CMT), (4) capacity headroom evidence, (5) receivable quality check. These gates are independent — clearing the tariff check does not clear UFLPA, capacity, or margin gates.
