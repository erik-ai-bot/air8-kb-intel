Supply chain intelligence · Sept 2026
Level 1 · The chain

A small Chenghai toy factory sells $2 plastic play sets to a US off-price chain. A commoditised, compliance-heavy product in a buyer-dominated market: fragmented Guangdong supply sells to a few off-price chains that buy on price, on-time delivery and quality, with compliance a must-have, not a selling point; low switching cost, a mature category trading down, sensitive to tariff and freight.

Band shrinking; he grows 86% inside it

2 · the price bands inside it — retail price tiers within toys (category reporting, not a customs split), against his own invoiced line

IntelligenceHis +86% is not demand — it is shelf space taken from another vendor, in the part of the market that is shrinking. Both markets are flat to down (US −22% in FY2025 and −3% YTD; EU +3% in 2025 and −4% in H1 2026) and the sub-$20 basic band is the declining part; the growth sits in the $30–70 licensed tier, already in Ross’s aisle via Spin Master and Jazwares and not his. So he is a share-taker in a declining band at a single buyer, on a price he could not raise: the volume grows, the position does not get stronger, and the gain reverses the moment the shelf goes to someone cheaper.
