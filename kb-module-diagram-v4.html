SUPPLIER SCORECARD — MODULE BREAKDOWN
Both Lead Scoring and Perf Scoring feed into the same Supplier Scorecard. The scorecard has 4 modules. Lead Scoring only uses Buyer-Supplier Pair as a proxy. Perf Scoring pre-screening uses QAQC + Performance + Buyer-Supplier Pair (if QAQC data is missing, it falls back to the remaining modules — not shown in this flow).
MODULE 1
QAQC
Quality assurance, compliance defects, audit history, and certification validity.
Perf Scor
MODULE 2
Performance
On-time delivery rate, lead-time accuracy, order completion ratio, and responsiveness.
Perf Scor
MODULE 3
Buyer-Supplier Pair
Historical match quality, category fit, volume alignment, and relationship tier.
Lead Scor Perf Scor
MODULE 4
Financial Health
Credit rating, financing history, paymentBehaviour, and risk band classification.
