Executive Summary · 3 Things the Boss Needs to Know

1. What happened

Bangladesh crossed the LDC income threshold in 2026, triggering the start of the EU EBA graduation clock — the 0% duty preference that BD has enjoyed for 30+ years will expire post-2029, replaced by the standard 12% EU MFN tariff.

2. Who it hits

~23 BD garment clients in the Air8 portfolio (7.2% of 320 total) are now on a 3-year countdown. The COMBO-002 segment (BD denim/woven EU-facing factories) bears the full exposure — currently ~$4B+ BD EU exports at risk of a 12% duty hit from 2029 onwards.

3. What Air8 does next

The engine scans every BD garment client's EU buyer concentration, flags the discount retailers most sensitive to cost increases (Primark, Lidl), and generates a working capital pre-positioning opportunity — buyers may accelerate orders into 2027-2028 ahead of the tariff cliff, creating a near-term WC financing window for factories.

Bottom line: The LDC graduation is a slow-burn tariff bomb — the real impact lands in 2029, but the buyer pre-positioning decisions that affect factory orders are happening right now. Air8's job is to catch that signal early.

AThe KB Layer Distinction (L1 vs L2)
L1 = Facts
L1 is the static knowledge base — rules, reference data, country facts, tariff schedules, pain point rules that don't change client to client. Think of it as "what the system knows about the world."

Example: "EU MFN for BD apparel = 12%. EBA = 0%." This is true for all BD garment factories. L1 fires first.
L2 = Context
L2 is the client-aware intelligence layer — it layers the L1 fact against specific client profiles, COMBO segments, and buyer concentration. L2 answers: "but what does this mean for this specific factory?"

Example: "COMBO-002 factories with >40% Primark/Lidl exposure face existential revenue pressure from order migration — the signal started in 2026." This is client-specific.
The flow: L1 tells you the rule. L2 tells you who it hurts most. Both fire in parallel during CP2 (Family Activation) — the output of Step 2 is a set of contextualized risk flags, not just raw facts.
