5 触发 Pain Point Chain：KB 里有对应的 pain points L2 · KB 数据
L2 做什么
KB 里有 pain points 库，COMBO 文件引用了它们。chemical_compliance 触发 PP-REG-REACH-001，再 cascade 到 universal pain points。
PP-REG-REACH-001
EU shipment contains SVHC >0.1% w/w → customs detention → buyer rejects → invoice disputed → re-process cost → DDP = Air8 importer liability → delisting risk
PP-001 · w:1.0
Cash flow gap on OA terms to EU buyers — goods in production, buyer pays after delivery; supplier must absorb re-test cost + delay
PP-003 · w:0.8
Buyer concentration — COMBO-005: Zara + H&M direct. If they pause, supplier loses majority revenue during test window
PP-TR-002 · w:1.0
TRY bank credit contraction — TRY-denominated lines reduced; cannot self-finance via local banks
PP-PK-001 · w:1.0
PK energy crisis — structural 10–20% production cost adder, on top of re-test cost (COMBO-009 only)

PP-REG-REACH-001.md · PP-001-cashflow-gap.md · PP-003-buyer-concentration.md · PP-TR-002-bank-credit-contraction.md · PP-PK-001-energy-crisis.md
6 拿具体规则：KB 告诉去哪里找 → 网上找具体数值 L2 · KB guidance + Web search
L2 做什么
KB 说去 ECHA 找 regulation 细节，去 buyer RSL 页找 limit。L2 web search 拿具体数值。
KB 告诉去哪里
REG-REACH.md — scope、SVHC threshold (0.1% w/w)、ECHA update cadence (Jan + Jul)、testing cost ($2,000–10,000/substance)、highest risk products (denim / children's apparel / home textile FR)
网上找具体值
ECHA: n-Hexane CAS 110-54-3 · BPAF CAS 1478-61-1 (group) · threshold >0.1% w/w · added Feb 2026 · SCIP deadline Aug 4 2026 · total SVHCs: 253
H&M RSL 2026: n-Hexane limit = 10 ppm — 100× stricter than REACH regulatory floor
关键发现
H&M buyer floor 10 ppm ≠ REACH 0.1% regulatory floor。Buyer RSL 通常比 REACH 严格得多。这个 gap = 供应商必须在 H&M floor 之下才能继续出货。
7 Apply 权重原则：KB 里有哪些 principles，权重多少 L1 · KB Principles
L1 做什么
KB 里的 PRINCIPLES.md 列出所有 reasoning principles 及权重。对 chemical_compliance + EU buyers，以下 principles 被激活。
PRIN-COMPLIANCE-COST
weight: 0.5 — Regulatory compliance is the primary cost driver. Re-test cost vs supplier size → severity scales with product specificity.
PRIN-BUYER-GEO-EXPOSURE
weight: 0.2 — Regulation jurisdiction is buyer-geography driven. EU-bound products trigger at full weight. US-only suppliers = not in scope.
PRIN-PRODUCT-TARIFF
weight: 0.3 — Regulation scope is product-category and material-specific. Denim (stonewash chemicals) = highest. Home textile (FR) = medium. General woven = lower.
RP-EVT-ENVCOMP-01
overlay — Environmental compliance → confirms active enforcement (new SVHC added = confirmed active). Not weight-based; qualifies/timeslices the signal.
KB 来源
PRINCIPLES.md — 里面每条 principle 有 activated_by 字段，chemical_compliance 属于 EVT-REGULATION，所以这三个 principle 同时激活

9 Link 到 WIP 节点：Production Stage × Compliance Action L2 · KB → WIP
L2 做什么
COMBO 产品类型 → 对应 production stage → 该 stage 的 compliance action。Denim 和 Home Textile 各在不同 stage 最危险。
WIP Stage 1
Sewing / Assembly — Audit BOM: denim 用什么 adhesive (n-Hexane)? home textile 用什么 coating (BPAF)? 确认有哪些物质在用，production 才能继续。
WIP Stage 2
Finishing / Wash — Stonewash/laser 用 n-Hexane solvent。如果 BOM 显示 >0.1% w/w → 停住，等 lab 结果。这里开始 14–21 天 production hold。
WIP Stage 3
QC / Pre-Ship — Lab 结果回来了。PASS → release。FAIL → 不能出货，进 Article 33 notification 流程。
WIP Stage 4
SCIP Submission — 如果 >0.1% w/w 已确认 → 向 ECHA 提交 SCIP，deadline Aug 4 2026。DDP = Air8 作为进口商承担这个义务。
