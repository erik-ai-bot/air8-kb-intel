📖 Glossary — Plain-English Terms

TermWhat it means
LF / Non-LFWhether the supplier or buyer has an existing relationship with the LF ecosystem
PairA specific supplier–buyer transactional link (from the Pair tab)
Bridge buyerA buyer connected to BOTH LF & Non-LF suppliers — fastest cross-sell entry point
Deal sizeThe $ amount of a single FR (financing request)
90th-percentile deal size9 out of 10 deals are smaller than this number
Long-tailMany small deals + a few very large ones; totals dominated by the extremes
Wallet stickinessWhether suppliers who joined early are still active years later
HHI (Concentration Index)0–10,000 score; sum of squared market shares × 10,000. Above 2,500 = highly concentrated
FR frequencyHow many FRs a supplier draws per active month
Joining-quarter cohortSuppliers grouped by the quarter they had their first FR
